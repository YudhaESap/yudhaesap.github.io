📑 Research

Herewith I post some of my recent research projects that made it into publications or conferences.

  • Predicting CRLM using TMUCRD Mapped-OMOP-OHDSI

    After we got feedback from practitioners, CRLM is more concerning than HCC. Thus, we pivoted to CRLM. Liver Metastases is one of the most prevalent malignancies that cause mortality among colorectal cancer patients where 25-30% affected by the malignancy worldwide. Predicting the occurrence of colorectal cancer liver metastases (CRLM) could benefit physicians in deciding the diagnoses. We created a prediction model using machine learning on electronic health records to discover susceptible characteristics for secondary malignant neoplasm of liver cancer in colorectal cancer patients.

  • Predicting Hepatic Carcinoma from Colorectal Cancer Patient using Machine Learning

    Liver is a common organ target of metastasis for colorectal cancer, which indicates the possibility of nonmetastatic cancer. We created a prediction model using machine learning on electronic health records to discover susceptible characteristics for liver cancer in colorectal cancer patients.

  • Bridging a Resilient Recovery Post-Pandemic through Digital Health Transformation

    Pandemic situations changed everything. In 2022, with the Indonesia Student Overseas Association, we created a brief on how digital health helps to cope with COVID-19 around certain countries.
